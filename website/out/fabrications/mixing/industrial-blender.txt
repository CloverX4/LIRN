3:T14ce,<h1 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 2rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.2;">Industrial Blenders</h1><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Batch and continuous mixing equipment for powders, granules, and paste-consistency materials. Uniform blending achieved through mechanical agitation inside a horizontal trough or shell — mixing times typically 3–15 minutes per batch.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;"><strong>Ribbon Blender</strong> — Horizontal U-trough with inner and outer helical ribbons rotating on a central shaft. Outer ribbon moves material inward, inner ribbon moves it outward — creates cross-flow mixing pattern. Most versatile design for general dry powder blending.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;"><strong>Paddle Blender</strong> — Multiple flat or angled paddles on a central shaft provide gentle tumbling action. Lower shear than ribbon type — preferred for fragile materials, granules, or products where particle size integrity must be maintained.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;"><strong>Plough-Share Blender</strong> — Plough-shaped mixing elements mounted on a central shaft inside a cylindrical drum. High-intensity mixing with optional chopper blades for breaking lumps. Fastest mixing time — suited for cohesive powders that resist conventional blending.</p><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.2;">Trough & Shell Design</h2><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Ribbon and paddle blenders use U-shaped troughs with close-tolerance clearance between ribbon tips and trough wall. Tighter clearance means less dead zone and better mixing — but requires precise fabrication.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Trough construction options:</p><ul style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem; margin-left: 1.5rem; color: #5a6e58;"><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Mild steel</strong> with food-grade epoxy lining for non-corrosive chemicals</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>SS 304</strong> for food, pharmaceutical, and mildly corrosive applications</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>SS 316</strong> for aggressive chemicals and chloride environments</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Polished interior</strong> (Ra < 0.8 μm) for pharmaceutical-grade cleanability</li></ul><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.2;">Drive & Sealing</h2><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Shaft-mounted gearbox drives with VFD for speed control. Shaft seals prevent material leakage and contamination ingress — packed gland seals for dry powders, mechanical seals for wet or pressurized applications.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Bearing housings are outboard-mounted to keep lubricant away from the product zone.
