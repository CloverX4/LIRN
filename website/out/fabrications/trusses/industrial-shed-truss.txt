Gusseted joints at truss nodes with high-strength friction-grip bolts. Base plates designed for anchor bolt patterns matching foundation design.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Column-to-truss connections use end plates or cleated angles depending on moment requirements.
