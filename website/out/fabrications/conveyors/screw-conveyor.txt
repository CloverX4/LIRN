The rotating helical flight moves material along a U-trough or tubular housing — dust-tight, compact, and self-metering.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;"><strong>U-Trough</strong> — Open-top trough with removable cover. Required for inclined transport above 20°, dusty materials, or applications demanding zero spillage. Centerless screw designs prevent material packing at the shaft.</p><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.2;">Flight Design</h2><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Flight configuration determines conveying behaviour:</p><ul style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem; margin-left: 1.5rem; color: #5a6e58;"><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Standard pitch</strong> (1x diameter) for general horizontal transport</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Short pitch</strong> (2/3 diameter) for inclined conveyors — prevents material rollback</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Variable pitch</strong> at inlet zones for uniform feeding from hoppers</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Cut flights</strong> for mixing action during transport</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Ribbon flights</strong> for sticky or fibrous materials that would wrap a solid flight</li></ul><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Flight thickness and edge hardening are specified based on material abrasiveness — critical for mineral processing where flight wear directly limits conveyor life.</p><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.2;">Bearing & Seal Design</h2><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Intermediate hanger bearings support the screw shaft at 3–4m intervals. Self-aligning bearings with replaceable bushings accommodate thermal expansion and shaft deflection.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">End seals prevent material leakage and dust emission.
