Longer conveyors or higher capacities use multiple-drive systems that distribute load along the belt, reducing tension and extending service life.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">VFD drives recommended for steep inclines (reduces startup shock), variable feed rates, and process control integration. Carry-side idlers every 3–5m, return-side every 1.5–2.0m. Wing pulleys on troughed conveyors maintain proper trough geometry.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Bearing failure is the primary cause of conveyor downtime — idler specification is critical to system reliability.</p><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.2;">Incline Design</h2><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Inclines above 10° require cleats (transverse ribs) to prevent material rollback.
