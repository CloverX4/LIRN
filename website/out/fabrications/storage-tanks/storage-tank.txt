Higher capacity per unit footprint — standard for large-volume storage. Cone bottoms for complete drainage of viscous or settled materials.</p><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;"><strong>Horizontal Tanks</strong> — Saddle-supported on steel or concrete cradles. Lower profile for height-restricted installations. Convenient for mobile or skid-mounted applications and where bottom access is needed.</p><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 1.5rem; color: #1a2e1a; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 2rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>SS 304</strong> for food-grade, pharmaceutical, and mildly corrosive service</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>SS 316</strong> for chloride-containing chemicals, seawater, and aggressive media</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Rubber or FRP lining</strong> for acid storage in mild steel shells</li></ul><p style="font-size: 14px; color: #5a6e58; line-height: 1.85; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Wall thickness calculated per ASME or API code for the design pressure, temperature, and corrosion allowance.</p><h2 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Agitator mounts</strong> — reinforced nozzles with bearing pads for top-entry mixers</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; line-height: 1.7;"><strong>Heating jackets</strong> — half-pipe or dimple jacket for maintaining product temperature</li><li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;
