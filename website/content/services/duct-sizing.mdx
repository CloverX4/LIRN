LIRN Solutions specializes in ductwork design and modification for dust collection systems. Proper duct sizing is critical for system efficiency, reduced maintenance, and compliance with environmental regulations.

# Duct Sizing & Corrective Action Planning

## Right-size your dust lines

Expert ductwork design and modification planning for optimal dust collection performance. Avoid backfires, high pressure losses, and system failures.

[Technical Sketch: Ductwork showing proper velocity transitions, expansion angles, and material flow]

## Design Principles

**Velocity Control** — 15–20 m/s for abrasive minerals (minimize erosion and settling)
**Transitions** — 35° included angle expansions prevent turbulence
**Access** — Inspection doors for maintenance without cutting ducts
**Supports** — Prevent sagging and vibration

## Common Issues Fixed

- **Undersized Ducts** → Backfires, excessive fan power, emissions
- **Poor Transitions** → Vortex formation, dust buildup
- **Inadequate Sizing** → System imbalance, bypass flow
