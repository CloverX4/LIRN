## Machine Types

**Horizontal Balers** — 50–500 kg/h, continuous auto operation, high density (600–900 kg/m³)
**Vertical Balers** — 20–200 kg/h, semi-auto, compact footprint, lower density (300–500 kg/m³)

## Selection Factors

- **Material Type** — PE/PP films, cardboard, metal, textiles
- **Volume** — Expected monthly tonnage
- **Space** — Available floor area and ceiling height
- **Integration** — Connection to existing conveyor/storage
- **Labor** — Full automation vs. semi-manual operation
- **ROI Timeline** — Typically 18–36 months payback
