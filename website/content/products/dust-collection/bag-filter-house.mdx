# Bag Filter Houses

## Industrial dust collection — air emissions compliance

Pulse-jet and shaker-type bag filters for capturing dust from kilns, crushers, conveyors, and furnaces. 99%+ collection efficiency with automated cleaning and 250°C+ rated fabrics.

[Product Image: Baghouse cross-section showing compartments, pulse tubes, and dust hopper]

**Pulse-Jet** — Automated high-pressure cleaning, handles high dust loads, continuous operation
**Shaker-Type** — Mechanical vibration, simpler operation, cost-effective, intermittent duty

## Engineering Specs

- **Collection Area**: 10–500 m²
- **Air Volume**: 500–50,000 m³/hr
- **Max Temperature**: 250°C (standard) / 400°C (ceramic)
- **Pressure Drop**: 100–150 mm WC at rated flow
- **Efficiency**: 99%+ for particles >5 microns
- **Fabrics**: Polyester (120°C), Nomex (250°C), PTFE (400°C)
- **Bag Life**: 18–36 months typical
- Pressure drop baseline verification
- Pulse timing and solenoid testing
- Temperature control calibration
- Leak detection under negative pressure (soap bubble test)
- 50-hour observation period with load documentation
- Operator training on alarms and maintenance procedures

## Maintenance Schedule

- Monthly: Check pulse solenoid operation and clean tube sheet
- Quarterly: Inspect bags visually for damage or calcite buildup
- Semi-annually: Check cage conditions, replace if warped
- Annually: Full service of pulse valve assembly, replace worn bags
