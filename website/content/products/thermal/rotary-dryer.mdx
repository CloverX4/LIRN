# Rotary Dryers

## Efficient moisture removal for powders and minerals

Co-current and counter-current dryers for drying minerals, chemicals, and clay products. Temperature range 50–350°C with precise moisture control and integrated dust collection.

[Product Image: Rotary dryer showing air flow pattern, heated shell, and moisture discharge]

**Co-Current** — Fast drying, lower energy cost, heat-sensitive materials
**Counter-Current** — Higher efficiency, fine powders, independent control

## Engineering Specs

- **Diameter**: 1.0–3.0m
- **Length**: 8–30m
- **Drying Temp**: 50–350°C
- **Speed**: 6–15 RPM
- **Throughput**: 0.5–50 TPH (material-dependent)
- **Air Volume**: 2,000–20,000 m³/hr
- **Moisture Removal**: Up to 60% initial moisture
- Induced draft fan sized for total system resistance
- Stack designed for proper dispersion

All equipment is factory pressure-tested before shipment.

## Control & Monitoring

The dryer is operated with:
- Temperature monitoring at multiple points via RTD sensors
- Mass flow meter on hot air inlet
- Moisture analyzer on product exit (optional)
- Data logging for batch documentation
- Emergency shutdown triggers for temperature exceedance

## Commissioning Procedure

Installation and commissioning steps include:
- Foundation verification and structural steel leveling
- Bearing alignment check
- Gas flow calibration and temperature profiling
- Test run with representative feedstock
- Data collection for 20-30 hours to establish steady-state drying curve
- Operator training on set-point adjustments and maintenance
