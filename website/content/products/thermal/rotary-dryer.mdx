# Rotary Dryers

[Product Image]

Continuous moisture removal for powders, minerals, and clay products. Temperature range 50–350°C with precise moisture control and integrated dust collection.

**Co-Current** — Hot gas and material travel in the same direction. Rapid initial drying with lower exit temperatures — preferred for heat-sensitive materials where over-drying causes degradation.

**Counter-Current** — Hot gas flows opposite to material. Higher thermal efficiency and lower residual moisture — preferred for minerals and aggregates where final dryness is critical.

## Internal Flight Design

Lifting flights inside the drum cascade material through the hot gas stream. Flight profile determines drying rate and residence time:
- **Straight flights** for coarse, free-flowing materials
- **Angled flights** for fine powders requiring longer air contact
- **Combination profiles** for materials with wide particle size distribution

Flight count, angle, and length are calculated from material bulk density and target moisture curve.

## Control & Monitoring

- RTD temperature sensors at multiple drum positions
- Mass flow metering on hot air inlet
- Optional moisture analyzer on product exit
- Data logging for batch documentation and process validation
- Emergency shutdown on temperature exceedance

## Commissioning

- Foundation verification and structural steel leveling
- Bearing alignment check and gas flow calibration
- Temperature profiling with test run on representative feedstock
- 20–30 hour data collection to establish steady-state drying curve
- Operator training on set-point adjustments and maintenance schedules

All equipment is factory pressure-tested before shipment.
