# Rotary Kilns

## Classic thermal processing equipment

Direct and indirect fired kilns for calcination, drying, and material transformation up to 1200°C. Refractory-lined rotating drums engineered for continuous 24/7 operation in cement, chemical, and mineral processing.

[Product Image: Rotary kiln cross-section showing refractory lining, burner, and discharge mechanism]

**Direct Fired** — Fuel burner inside drum, maximum heat transfer
**Indirect Fired** — External shell heating, sensitive material handling

## Engineering Specs

- **Diameter**: 1.5–3.5m
- **Length**: 15–45m  
- **Temperature**: Up to 1200°C
- **Speed**: 4–12 RPM
- **Lining**: Refractory brick or ceramic fiber
- **Drive**: Belt, gear, or VFD options
- **Residence Time**: 30–90 minutes (material dependent)

We provide complete installation supervision including:
- Foundation design and concrete pour verification
- Roller and bearing alignment to within 0.5mm TIR
- Refractory lining dry-out procedure (critical for long kiln life)
- Burner calibration and temperature control commissioning
- 40-hour burn-in run with load monitoring

## Maintenance & Spare Parts

Standard maintenance includes:
- Bearing lubrication every 250 hours
- Tyrebolts inspection and re-tensioning at 1000-hour intervals
- Refractory buildup cleaning every 3-6 months depending on material
- Burner nozzle replacement annually

We maintain spare parts inventory for all critical components including roller bearings, seals, and refractory materials.
