# Industrial Ducts

Custom rectangular and round ductwork for kiln exhausts, baghouse connections, and process gas systems. Sized for optimal velocity (15–20 m/s for abrasive dust) to minimize pressure loss and erosion.

**Rectangular** — Space-efficient, easy access
**Round** — Lower pressure drop, better flow distribution

- **Diameter**: 300–2000mm customizable
- **Materials**: Galvanized steel, SS 304/316, carbon steel
- **Temperature**: Up to 600°C service
- **Connections**: Flanged, welded, or quick-lock
- **Accessories**: Expansion joints, support brackets, access doors
