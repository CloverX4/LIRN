# Chain Conveyors

LIRN Fabrications specializes in chain conveyors designed for heavy-duty material handling. Our en-masse and drag chain conveyors provide reliable transport of abrasive and high-temperature materials.

## Types

- Moving flight design
- High capacity operation
- Gentle material handling

### Drag Chain Conveyors

- Various flight configurations
- Customizable for specific applications

## Key Features

- Robust chain and flight construction
- Wear-resistant materials
- Easy maintenance and inspection
- Multiple drive options

## Applications

- Cement and lime processing
- Power generation
- Mining and minerals
- Chemical processing
- Waste handling
