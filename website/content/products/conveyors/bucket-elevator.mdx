# Bucket Elevators

LIRN Fabrications provides bucket elevators for efficient vertical transport of bulk materials. Our elevators are designed for high capacity and reliable operation in demanding industrial environments.

## Types

### Centrifugal Discharge Elevators
- High-speed operation
- Suitable for free-flowing materials
- Capacities up to 500 m³/h

### Continuous Discharge Elevators
- Low-speed operation
- Gentle material handling
- Ideal for fragile materials

## Key Features

- Heavy-duty construction
- Dust-tight casings
- Easy maintenance access
- Explosion-proof options available

## Applications

- Grain handling
- Cement plants
- Mining operations
- Chemical processing
- Food industry

## Technical Specifications

- Heights up to 50 meters
- Various bucket sizes and materials
- Operating temperatures up to 150°C
- Multiple drive configurations
