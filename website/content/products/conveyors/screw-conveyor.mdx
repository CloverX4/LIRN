# Screw Conveyors

LIRN Fabrications offers high-quality screw conveyors designed for reliable material handling in various industrial applications. Our screw conveyors provide efficient, low-maintenance transport of bulk materials.

## Types

### Horizontal Screw Conveyors
- U-trough and tubular designs
- Multiple inlet/outlet configurations
- Variable pitch for different materials

### Inclined Screw Conveyors
- Up to 45° incline angles
- Centerless designs to prevent material fallback
- Custom flight configurations

## Key Features

- Robust construction with heavy-duty bearings
- Easy access for maintenance
- Dust-tight enclosures
- Variable speed drives

## Applications

- Cement and concrete plants
- Food processing
- Chemical processing
- Mining and minerals
- Agriculture

## Technical Specifications

- Capacities up to 200 m³/h
- Lengths up to 20 meters
- Operating temperatures up to 200°C
- Various screw diameters available
