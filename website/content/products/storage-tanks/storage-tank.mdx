# Storage Tanks

[Product Image]

Atmospheric and pressure storage vessels for chemicals, water, fuels, and process intermediates. Every tank is custom-designed for the stored medium — material selection, wall thickness, and internals are driven by the specific application.

**Vertical Tanks** — Cone or flat bottom on concrete ring foundation. Higher capacity per unit footprint — standard for large-volume storage. Cone bottoms for complete drainage of viscous or settled materials.

**Horizontal Tanks** — Saddle-supported on steel or concrete cradles. Lower profile for height-restricted installations. Convenient for mobile or skid-mounted applications and where bottom access is needed.

## Material Selection

Material choice is driven by the stored medium:
- **Mild steel with epoxy lining** for water, fuel oil, and non-corrosive chemicals
- **SS 304** for food-grade, pharmaceutical, and mildly corrosive service
- **SS 316** for chloride-containing chemicals, seawater, and aggressive media
- **Rubber or FRP lining** for acid storage in mild steel shells

Wall thickness calculated per ASME or API code for the design pressure, temperature, and corrosion allowance.

## Internals & Accessories

- **Agitator mounts** — reinforced nozzles with bearing pads for top-entry mixers
- **Heating jackets** — half-pipe or dimple jacket for maintaining product temperature
- **Baffles** — vertical plates to improve mixing efficiency and prevent vortex formation
- **Level instrumentation** — sight glass, float gauge, or ultrasonic level transmitter
- **Manways** — 500mm or 600mm diameter for internal inspection access

## Fabrication & Testing

Tanks are fabricated in-shop where possible and transported as complete units. Larger tanks are site-fabricated from rolled plate with field welding.

All tanks undergo:
- Radiographic examination of critical welds (per code requirements)
- Hydrostatic pressure test at 1.5x design pressure
- Leak test on all nozzle connections
- Surface preparation and coating before dispatch

## Foundation Design

Foundation recommendations provided with each tank — ring wall for vertical tanks, saddle piers for horizontal. Proper foundation prevents shell distortion that causes premature failure and leak paths.
